SECTION 1: ABOUT THE COLLEGE
1.1 Introduction to Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management
Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management, popularly known as GITM, is a premier multi-disciplinary institution of higher education situated in the heart of West Bengal. Established in the year 2005, the college has grown exponentially over the past two decades to become one of the most sought-after educational destinations in the eastern region of India. The institution is affiliated with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), West Bengal, and is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. The college is also recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and holds accreditation from the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a B++ grade.

Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management is spread across a lush green campus of approximately 25 acres located in Barasat, North 24 Parganas, West Bengal. The campus is well-connected to Kolkata city, being approximately 25 kilometers from the city center, and is easily accessible by road, rail, and bus. The institute is committed to providing world-class education at affordable costs, making higher education accessible to students from all socioeconomic backgrounds, including those from rural areas, tribal communities, and economically weaker sections of society.

The vision of Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management is to create a knowledge-driven, innovation-oriented academic environment that nurtures future-ready professionals, researchers, and entrepreneurs. The mission of the institution is to deliver quality technical and professional education through experienced faculty, modern infrastructure, industry collaboration, and a strong value system. The college believes that education is not merely the transfer of information but the holistic development of a student's personality, critical thinking ability, ethical values, and professional competence.

Since its inception, GITM has produced thousands of graduates who have gone on to work in some of the most reputed organizations across India and abroad. The college has a strong alumni network spread across various sectors including Information Technology, Healthcare, Agriculture, Banking, Management, and Manufacturing. The placement cell of the institution works round the clock to ensure that every eligible student gets a fair opportunity to secure a rewarding career.

The college currently offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs across multiple disciplines including Engineering, Technology, Science, Commerce, Management, Agriculture, Pharmacy, and Allied Health Sciences. With over 5000 students enrolled across various programs and more than 300 faculty and staff members, GITM is a vibrant, diverse, and dynamic institution that celebrates academic excellence and cultural richness in equal measure.

The college has a dedicated Research and Development cell that encourages faculty members and students to engage in applied research, patent development, and publication of research papers in reputed national and international journals. The R&D cell has collaborations with several industries and research organizations, providing students and faculty members with exposure to real-world challenges and cutting-edge technologies.

GITM takes pride in its inclusive culture, where students from different states, communities, religions, and economic backgrounds come together to learn, grow, and create. The college has a zero-tolerance policy against ragging and discrimination and maintains a safe, respectful, and nurturing environment for all its students. The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), Anti-Ragging Committee, and Student Grievance Redressal Cell are always active and accessible to address any concern that students may have during their time at the institution.

SECTION 3: FEES STRUCTURE — DETAILED DEPARTMENT WISE
IMPORTANT GENERAL INFORMATION ABOUT FEES
Before going into the department-wise fee breakdown, here is some important general information that every student and parent must be aware of:

Total Program Duration:

BTech programs: 4 years (8 semesters)
BSc Agriculture: 4 years (8 semesters)
BPharm: 4 years (8 semesters)
BBA: 3 years (6 semesters)
BCA: 3 years (6 semesters)
BMLT: 3 years (6 semesters) + 6 months internship
Fee Payment Schedule:

Fees are to be paid on a per-semester basis
Each academic year has 2 semesters
Semester fees are due at the beginning of each semester, before classes commence
A grace period of 15 days is provided from the date of fee notice for payment
Late payment attracts a fine of Rs. 50 per day after the grace period
Payment Modes Available:

Online Payment — Through the college's official online portal (www.gitm.ac.in/fees), using Net Banking, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Debit Card, or Credit Card. Students receive an instant digital receipt after successful payment. Online payment is available 24x7.
Demand Draft / Cheque Payment — Students can submit a crossed demand draft or cheque drawn in favor of "Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management" payable at Barasat. Cheques must be submitted to the Accounts Department between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM on working days. Receipt is issued after cheque clearance, which typically takes 2-3 working days.
Cash Payment — Cash payment is accepted at the college Accounts Department (Ground Floor, Administrative Block, Room No. 102) between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM on all working days (Monday to Saturday, except second Saturdays and public holidays). Students must carry their college ID card and admission receipt at the time of cash payment. Cash receipt is issued immediately.
One-Time Payments (Non-Refundable):
Certain charges are collected only once at the time of admission and are non-refundable under any circumstances. These are clearly mentioned in the fee structure of each department below.

Refund Policy:

If a student withdraws from the program before the commencement of classes, 90% of the tuition fee is refunded (admission fee and other one-time charges are not refunded).
If withdrawal is made within 15 days of commencement of classes, 80% of the tuition fee is refunded.
If withdrawal is made after 15 days but before 30 days, 50% of tuition fee is refunded.
No refund is applicable after 30 days of commencement of classes.

IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT FACULTY
Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management takes immense pride in its dedicated and highly qualified faculty team. The institution has more than 130 full-time faculty members across all departments, along with visiting faculty and industry experts who regularly engage with students. A large proportion of our faculty members hold PhD degrees from reputed universities including IITs, NITs, Jadavpur University, Calcutta University, and other prestigious institutions. Many faculty members are actively engaged in research, consultancy, and publishing in reputed national and international journals. The student-to-faculty ratio at GITM is maintained at approximately 15:1, ensuring that every student receives adequate individual attention and mentorship.

SECTION 5: SCHOLARSHIPS
Overview of Scholarship Programs at GITM
Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management firmly believes that financial constraints should never be a barrier to quality education. The institution has therefore established a robust scholarship ecosystem that provides financial assistance to deserving students from various backgrounds. Scholarships at GITM are awarded on the basis of academic merit, financial need, community affiliation, and geographical background. The scholarship amounts are calculated as a percentage of the tuition fee component of the semester fee.

Important Note: Scholarship amounts are applied to the tuition fee component only of the semester fee. Other components like examination fee, laboratory fee, development fee, etc., remain payable by the student even if they receive a scholarship.

For reference, the tuition fee components are as follows (these are the amounts on which scholarship percentages are calculated):

BTech CSE: Rs. 42,000 per semester
BTech CSE AIML: Rs. 48,000 per semester
BTech CSE Data Science: Rs. 46,000 per semester
BTech Mechanical: Rs. 40,000 per semester
BTech Electrical: Rs. 39,000 per semester
BTech ECE: Rs. 40,000 per semester
BTech Civil: Rs. 38,000 per semester
BSc Agriculture: Rs. 28,000 per semester
BPharm: Rs. 36,000 per semester
BBA: Rs. 22,000 per semester
BCA: Rs. 24,000 per semester
BMLT: Rs. 26,000 per semester
The following scholarships are awarded by the institution from its own scholarship fund. In addition to these, students may also apply for various government scholarships which are described in a later section.

SCHOLARSHIP 1: GREENWOOD MERIT EXCELLENCE SCHOLARSHIP (GMES)
Scholarship Name: Greenwood Merit Excellence Scholarship (GMES)

Objective: This scholarship is awarded to recognize and reward exceptional academic performance at the Higher Secondary (Class 12) or equivalent board examination level. The college believes in identifying bright young minds and supporting them financially so that financial constraints do not prevent meritorious students from pursuing their dream programs.

Eligibility Criteria:

The applicant must have secured a minimum of 85% aggregate marks in the Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent board examination (West Bengal Board / CBSE / ICSE / any State Board recognized by COBSE)
The applicant must have taken admission to any undergraduate program at GITM in the first year
The scholarship is applicable from the very first semester
The student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 8.0 out of 10 (or equivalent percentage of 75%) in each semester at GITM to continue receiving this scholarship in subsequent semesters
If a student's CGPA falls below 8.0 in any semester, the scholarship will be suspended for the following semester but can be reinstated if the CGPA is restored
There is no income limit for this scholarship — it is purely merit-based
The scholarship is applicable to students from West Bengal as well as students from other states
Scholarship Amount: 75% waiver on tuition fee per semester

Scholarship Amount Example (BTech CSE AIML):

Tuition Fee per semester: Rs. 48,000
Scholarship Amount: 75% of Rs. 48,000 = Rs. 36,000 per semester
Tuition Fee payable after scholarship: Rs. 12,000 per semester
Renewable: Yes — renewed every semester based on GITM academic performance (minimum CGPA 8.0)

Application Process: Submit self-attested copies of Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets, admit card, and income certificate (if any) to the Scholarship Cell, Administrative Block, Room 105, within 30 days of admission.

Number of Seats: Maximum 15 seats per academic year across all programs combined (awarded to top merit rankers)

SCHOLARSHIP 2: GREENWOOD ACHIEVERS SCHOLARSHIP (GAS)
Scholarship Name: Greenwood Achievers Scholarship (GAS)

Objective: This scholarship is designed for students who have performed very well in their board examinations but may not have achieved the 85% threshold required for the GMES scholarship. It provides meaningful financial support to strong performers and motivates them to continue excelling in their academic journey at GITM.

Eligibility Criteria:

The applicant must have secured 75% to 84.99% aggregate marks in the Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent board examination
The applicant must have taken admission to any undergraduate program at GITM in the first year
The student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.0 out of 10 in each semester at GITM to continue receiving this scholarship in subsequent semesters
Applicable to all students from West Bengal, other states, and other countries
No income restriction — purely merit-based
Scholarship Amount: 50% waiver on tuition fee per semester

Scholarship Amount Example (BTech Mechanical Engineering):

Tuition Fee per semester: Rs. 40,000
Scholarship Amount: 50% of Rs. 40,000 = Rs. 20,000 per semester
Tuition Fee payable after scholarship: Rs. 20,000 per semester
Renewable: Yes — renewed every semester based on GITM academic performance (minimum CGPA 7.0)

Application Process: Submit board marksheets and completed scholarship application form to Scholarship Cell within 30 days of admission.

Number of Seats: Maximum 30 seats per academic year across all programs combined

SCHOLARSHIP 3: GREENWOOD SOCIAL WELFARE SCHOLARSHIP — ECONOMICALLY WEAKER SECTION (GSWSEWS)
Scholarship Name: Greenwood Social Welfare Scholarship — Economically Weaker Section (GSWS-EWS)

Objective: This scholarship is meant for students coming from economically disadvantaged families where the financial condition may make it extremely difficult for the student to continue higher education. GITM is committed to ensuring that no student has to abandon their educational dreams due to poverty.

Eligibility Criteria:

The applicant's annual family income must be below Rs. 2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Rupees) per annum from all sources combined
The applicant must submit a valid Income Certificate issued by the Block Development Officer (BDO) or the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) or any other competent authority recognized by the Government of West Bengal (or respective state government)
The applicant must have secured at least 55% marks in the Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent board examination
The student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 6.0 out of 10 in each semester at GITM to continue receiving this scholarship
The applicant must not be receiving any other scholarship that amounts to more than 50% of the tuition fee; if receiving other scholarships, the combined scholarship amount should not exceed the full tuition fee
Applicable to students from all communities and all states
Scholarship Amount: 60% waiver on tuition fee per semester

Scholarship Amount Example (BCA):

Tuition Fee per semester: Rs. 24,000
Scholarship Amount: 60% of Rs. 24,000 = Rs. 14,400 per semester
Tuition Fee payable after scholarship: Rs. 9,600 per semester
Renewable: Yes — the income certificate must be renewed every academic year. CGPA must be maintained at or above 6.0.

Application Process: Submit income certificate (not older than 6 months), ration card copy or BPL card copy (if applicable), marksheets, admission documents, and completed scholarship form to the Scholarship Cell.

Number of Seats: Up to 40 students per academic year (combined across all programs)

Annual family income must be below Rs. 4,00,000 (Four Lakh Rupees) per annum

Applicable to students from all communities

Government Scholarships Available to GITM Students
Beyond the institutional scholarships described above, students at GITM are also eligible to apply for several prestigious government-funded scholarship programs. The Scholarship Cell actively assists students in applying for these government scholarships and maintaining their renewals. The following government scholarship programs are available to eligible GITM students:

Swami Vivekananda Merit Cum Means Scholarship (SVMCM) — Government of West Bengal
The Swami Vivekananda Merit Cum Means Scholarship (SVMCM) is one of the most prestigious and widely availed scholarships for students pursuing higher education in West Bengal. This scholarship is entirely funded by the Government of West Bengal through the Department of Higher Education and is administered through the online portal e-scholarships.wb.gov.in. We strongly encourage every eligible student at GITM to apply for this scholarship as it can significantly reduce the financial burden of higher education.

About the SVMCM Scholarship:

The SVMCM scholarship was launched by the Government of West Bengal with the noble objective of ensuring that financial constraints do not prevent meritorious students from accessing quality higher education. It is available to students from West Bengal who are pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate programs in technical, professional, and general stream institutions.

Eligibility for SVMCM (for new applicants at GITM):

The student must be a permanent resident of West Bengal with West Bengal as their domicile state
The student must have passed the West Bengal Higher Secondary Examination (HS) or any recognized equivalent examination conducted within West Bengal
The student must have secured a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in the qualifying examination (HS or equivalent)
The annual family income of the applicant must not exceed Rs. 2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Rupees) per annum from all sources
The student must be enrolled in a government-approved institution affiliated with a recognized university — GITM qualifies under this criterion as it is affiliated with MAKAUT and approved by AICTE and UGC
The student must not be receiving any other full scholarship from the government or any other source that covers tuition fees in full
Scholarship Amount under SVMCM:

The SVMCM scholarship provides the following amounts for technical and professional courses:

Category	Scholarship Amount per Annum
Technical/Professional Courses (BTech, BPharm, etc.)	Rs. 60,000 per year (i.e., Rs. 30,000 per semester)
General / Non-Technical UG Courses (BBA, BCA, BMLT, BSc, etc.)	Rs. 30,000 per year (i.e., Rs. 15,000 per semester)
Note: These amounts are as per the latest government notification and are subject to revision by the Government of West Bengal from time to time. Students are advised to check the official SVMCM portal for the most up-to-date scholarship amounts.

SVMCM Application Process:

Visit the official website: https://svmcm.wbhed.gov.in or https://scholarships.wbhed.gov.in
Register using your Aadhaar number and mobile number
Fill in the online application form with all required details
Upload the required documents:
Scanned copy of HS Marksheet and Certificate
Income Certificate from competent authority (not older than 1 year)
Bank passbook copy (student's own bank account — preferably in a nationalized bank)
Aadhaar Card
Passport-size photograph
Residential certificate / voter ID / ration card showing West Bengal address
Admission receipt from GITM
Fee payment receipt from GITM
Submit the application online and note the application reference number
The GITM Scholarship Cell will verify your application at the institutional level before forwarding it to the university and the government portal
Important Deadlines: The SVMCM application window typically opens in September-October for the academic year beginning in July. Students must not miss the deadline. The exact deadline is announced by the Government of West Bengal each year and is displayed on the scholarship portal and the college notice board and website.

Renewal of SVMCM:

Existing scholars must renew their SVMCM scholarship every year by submitting their annual marksheet/result showing they have cleared all papers without any back papers
A minimum of 60% marks (or CGPA equivalent) must be maintained in each year/semester
Income certificate must be renewed and resubmitted each year
Renewal application is done online on the same portal
The Scholarship Cell at GITM conducts dedicated SVMCM application workshops at the beginning of each academic year, where students receive step-by-step guidance on how to apply online, what documents to prepare, and how to avoid common mistakes that lead to rejection or delays. We strongly encourage all West Bengal domicile students from economically weaker families to attend these workshops.

The SVMCM scholarship, when combined with GITM's institutional scholarships (where applicable), can make the cost of higher education at GITM remarkably affordable. For example, a BTech student at GITM receiving both the GSWSEWS institutional scholarship (60% tuition fee waiver = approximately Rs. 25,200 per semester for BTech CSE) AND the SVMCM scholarship (Rs. 30,000 per semester for technical courses) could potentially have their entire tuition fee and more covered in a given semester, making higher education virtually free for the most eligible students.

Other Government Scholarships Applicable to GITM Students:
Post Matric Scholarship for SC Students (Government of West Bengal / Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India):
Students belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC) who are studying in post-matriculation courses including BTech, BCA, BBA, BMLT, BSc Agriculture, and BPharm at GITM are eligible to apply for the Post Matric Scholarship for SC students. Students from Other Backward Classes (OBC) enrolled in post-matriculation programs at GITM may apply for the OBC Post Matric Scholarship through the West Bengal e-scholarship portal. Central Sector Scholarship Scheme for College and University Students (Ministry of Education, Government of India):
This scholarship is for students who secured above the 80th percentile in their Class 12 board exams and whose family income is below Rs. 8,00,000 per annum. The scholarship provides Rs. 12,000 per annum for the first three years and Rs. 20,000 per annum for professional programs. Applications are made through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

Minority Scholarship Schemes:
Students from minority communities (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi) enrolled at GITM may apply for scholarships under the Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarship Schemes for Minorities administered by the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India, through the National Scholarship Portal. Income limit is Rs. 2,00,000 per annum.

Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme for Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) and Railway Protection Force (RPF):
Children of personnel serving in or who died or were disabled while serving in CAPF or RPF are eligible for this scholarship. Amount: Rs. 25,000 per annum for boys and Rs. 30,000 per annum for girls studying in technical and professional programs at GITM. Apply through NSP.

Ishan Uday Scholarship for North East Region:
Students from the eight northeastern states (Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura) enrolled at GITM are eligible to apply for the Ishan Uday Scholarship provided by the University Grants Commission. Overview of Placement at GITM
The Training and Placement Cell (TPC) of Greenwood Institute of Technology and Management is one of the most active and result-oriented departments of the college. The TPC is headed by Dr. Suvendu Roy, a seasoned professional with 12 years of industry experience at Infosys and Wipro before joining academia, and it is supported by a dedicated team of placement coordinators, training managers, and industry liaison officers.

The placement journey at GITM begins from the very first year of the program. First-year students undergo a structured Career Readiness Program (CRP) that focuses on developing communication skills, resume writing, aptitude and reasoning skills, coding abilities, and interview preparation. From the second year onwards, students undergo domain-specific technical training, workshops by industry experts, hackathons, coding competitions, and mock interviews. By the time they reach their final year, GITM students are thoroughly prepared to face campus recruitment processes of top companies.

The college has strong relationships with over 150+ companies across sectors including Information Technology, Manufacturing, Pharmaceuticals, Healthcare, BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance), Agribusiness, and Management Consulting, all of whom participate in GITM's campus recruitment process every year.

The placement season at GITM officially begins in August-September of the final year (4th year for BTech / BSc Agriculture / BPharm students, and 3rd year for BBA / BCA / BMLT students). Companies begin registering with the TPC from June onwards. Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) may be extended from July onwards for students who have completed internships with companies.
